Removing intermediaries and settlement delays could also eliminate mechanisms that financial institutions rely on to manage liquidity  distribute risk and maintain market stability.That tension was at the center of a discussion between Beatrice Fares  ex-directorate general for Financial Stability  Financial Services and Capital Markets Union (DG FISMA)  European Commission  and Peter Kerstens  adviser at the European Commission  hosted by Competition Policy International (CPI)  a PYMNTS company.“With the tokenization  trading  clearing and settlement will be included in one single step ” Fares said  adding that this consolidation  enabled by programmable transactions and shared ledgers  could shift trust away from established intermediaries and toward the underlying infrastructure and smart contracts.When Faster Settlement Changes the Economics of LiquidityTraditional financial digitalization made trading and clearing faster without changing the institutional architecture supporting them. Tokenization introduces a different proposition by promising to bring trading  clearing and settlement onto shared digital infrastructure  potentially eliminating much of the operational complexity that has defined securities markets for decades.But the implications extend beyond operational efficiency.Today’s settlement delays provide institutions with time to source securities  arrange financing  net obligations and perform compliance checks. Atomic settlement  in which the exchange of assets and payment occurs simultaneously  could substantially reduce counterparty exposure while removing that flexibility.“From  in a way  post-trade  post-financing your trades  you now will have to almost pre-finance your trades ” Kerstens said.For banks  brokers and institutional investors  the distinction matters. Instantaneous settlement can reduce the need for intraday credit  but requiring liquidity to be available immediately may create new pressures during periods of market stress. Rather than eliminating financial risk  tokenization could change where and when institutions encounter it.New Technology  Same Old Financial Giants?Tokenization also promises to challenge the established infrastructure providers that dominate securities clearing and settlement. But Fares warned that the same network effects protecting today’s incumbents could reappear on blockchain-based infrastructure.“The same network  network effects will come back on shared ledgers ” she said.Platforms attracting major banks  issuers and custodians accumulate liquidity  improve pricing and attract additional participants. That creates a self-reinforcing advantage that smaller competitors may struggle to overcome.Established providers are hardly standing still.
