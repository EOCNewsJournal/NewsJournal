Artprice supports this development  which enables authors to develop their work without first having to wait for an intermediary to recognize its value.At 64  thierry Ehrmann  founder of Artprice  the world leader in art market information for nearly thirty years  is a writer  a sculptor and visual artist with nearly forty-five years of practice  and the creator of La Demeure du Chaos. The work has received official recognition from the French state as a ""total work of art  on a par with the Palais Idéal du Facteur Cheval and Niki de Saint Phalle's Cyclop  "" as stated by the Minister of Culture on March 20  2025  and by the Ministry of Culture in the Senate edition of the Official Journal on December 18  2025.His longstanding work as a writer encompasses numerous books  treatises  essays  short works  manifestos  and catalogues raisonnés devoted in particular to the history of art and its market. These include Le Code des ventes volontaires et judiciaires   a legal analysis in comparative European law and a sociological study spanning 1 800 pages  tracing  in particular  the history of auctioneers from 1525 to the reform enacted by the law of July 10  2000  and the evolution of their profession.The 504-page Opus IX of La Demeure du Chaos  published by Éditions Musée L'Organe  is one of the works in this body of writing. Its free digital edition has exceeded 32 million downloads  as formally documented by a French judicial officer ( huissier de justice ).For thierry Ehrmann  writing and the visual arts are part of the same inquiry  one that long predates his dialogue with artificial intelligence. As early as 1987  he was a pioneer of AI-generated computer imagery.This position also draws on nearly thirty years of international experience in publishing  book sales representation  and distribution. Artprice's acquisitions of publishing houses and publishing catalogs in France  Europe  and the United States have given him firsthand knowledge of the lives of books and their authors.This debate extends his commitment to freedom of artistic creation. Through La Demeure du Chaos/Abode of Chaos (as The New York Times calls it)  thierry Ehrmann was personally and actively involved in the work that led to the law of July 7  2016.Indeed  in his memorable and historic argument before an appellate court  he raised the inadequacy of the legislative framework protecting freedom of artistic creation. He thereby contributed to the legislative debate that accompanied its explicit recognition in the law of July 7  2016 (LCAP)  whose first article states: ""Artistic creation is free.""Until that date  for 135 years  this freedom had been the exclusive preserve of the press  publishing  and printing under the law of July 29  1881. His defense of authors working with AI pursues the same imperative: to preserve their ability to determine the meaning  form  and terms of dissemination of their work.The cosa mentale provides the starting point for this reflection. From Leonardo da Vinci to Marcel Duchamp  it affirms the primacy of conception in the work. Authors bring a cosmovision : a singular way of perceiving the world through their experiences  knowledge  and contradictions. Whether writers or visual artists  they choose the means through which that thought takes form.The initial intention may become more precise as the work progresses. An objection  a discovery in the source material  or a new construction may lead authors to revise their starting point. Their freedom lies in their ability to decide on these transformations and recognize their meaning. They direct the work through this continuing sequence of choices  revisions  and rejections.AI gives authors tools whose use is not contingent on a promise of publication. In its role as a writing assistant  it does not pay their royalties  offer them a publishing advance  or decide whether their work will be distributed. Authors can discuss its proposals without simultaneously negotiating with the party that controls their publishing income.thierry Ehrmann considers a monthly premium subscription budget of €100 to €200 at the very most to be an extremely modest cost in relation to the research and writing resources it provides and the time it saves. As an investment in an individual working tool  this expense opens up possibilities that previously required far greater resources.The freedom to revisit the work is a decisive contribution. At one in the morning  at five  or in the afternoon  authors can reopen their writing laboratory  request several alternatives  or rebuild chapters eight through eleven because a new idea changes the perspective.They can reject every proposal  return to an earlier version  and continue until they find the ""forming form"" they have been seeking in vain  in the sense of Luigi Pareyson's celebrated theory.This availability encourages exacting standards. Authors can test an objection  ask why a sequence seems weak  and compare different formulations. They also learn to specify what they expect and identify what they reject. AI's research capabilities help them explore a corpus  draw connections between texts  prepare a chronology  or identify contradictions. Source verification by AI saves authors considerable time compared with the many individual searches they would otherwise have had to conduct during their preparatory work using search engines flooded with advertising links.Their freedom also includes the choice of assistants. They can compare several AI systems  assign particular tasks to each  and switch tools when a limitation does not suit their project. They assess these systems' alignment by comparing their rules and responses with their own experience. The range of services and the options for adapting certain open-weight models broaden their choices.This autonomy has psychological and anthropological significance. Throughout his career  thierry Ehrmann has known authors for whom requesting an advance or submitting a manuscript was a source of intense apprehension. Several authors have recently confided to him that the debate opened up around AI offers them a chance to catch their breath.Being able to present an idea that is still fragile and request rigorous criticism  without immediately putting publication  credibility  or income at stake  changes the conditions in which authors work.Experience in publishing gives this reflection a concrete dimension. Among the publishing houses acquired by Artprice is Sound View Press  Peter Hastings Falk's American publishing house  acquired in 2000 and known in particular for Who Was Who in American Art and some fifty books on art history. These numerous acquisitions in the United States and Europe encompassed print publications  biographies  catalogues raisonnés  reference collections  and works of sociology. Through Artprice and Groupe Serveur  thierry Ehrmann's publishing experience also extends to cultural  sociological  legal  and scientific publications.As 1999 gave way to 2000  the shift to the internet was opening up a new horizon. Print publishing remained predominant  and limited bandwidth made the online dissemination of lengthy books and large databases virtually impossible. Many professionals doubted the economic prospects of this new means of dissemination.It was in this context that thierry Ehrmann negotiated  particularly abroad  the termination  in exchange for compensation  of exclusive distribution agreements associated with the publishing houses and catalogs that had been acquired. These agreements made it possible to regain control over their dissemination and organize their digital development. At the time  several distributors preferred immediate compensation to the prospect of internet revenues in which they had little faith  or which they regarded as utopian.These negotiations gave him direct experience of the relationships among authors  publishers  book sales representatives  and distributors in countries with differing contracts and distribution rules. They involved the value of the catalogs  the marketing work already undertaken  the authors' reputations  and the possibility of choosing new paths. This knowledge was built over time through engagement with the international realities of the book industry and the high-profile bankruptcies of major distributors  such as the Vilo distribution group in 2004  which led to extreme market concentration.The autonomy AI provides can now transform the relationship with a publisher. Authors who have a more fully developed text  several versions  and reasons to support their choices can enter the collaboration with greater decision-making power. An editor's perspective retains its value when it contributes to the work and the intervention is understood and accepted. The aim is to enable authors to choose this support on their own authority  on more balanced terms.Publishing  sales representation  and distribution form a chain whose structure affects access to the public. When the assessment of a manuscript  the prospect of publication  and anticipated payments are intertwined  the cost of disagreement can be high. Having additional resources with which to work and explore other paths to publication changes the terms of this negotiation.History reminds us of what can be at stake when a text is altered. The cuts to Violette Leduc's Ravages in 1955 and Raymond Carver's request in July 1980 to halt production of a substantially reworked collection shed light on conflicts over control of a work. The author's consent remains at the heart of the editorial relationship.A reading inspired by Hegel offers a way to examine this relationship among dependence  work  and recognition. Those who produce also discover  through their activity  the means to assert their own capabilities. Authors can acknowledge the contributions of those they work with while retaining the initiative in their own work.The experience of Dialogue Between a Thinker and AI  subtitled The Great Revenge of Literary Minds and Humanists in a Digital Age  illustrates this way of organizing the work. thierry Ehrmann led the conception of the book through a sustained dialogue with ChatGPT 5.6 Sol Pro  under his direction as its author  while using Gemini Ultra DeepSeek (Google) for specific research  occasional checks  or peripheral or strictly subsidiary questions. This division of tasks preserved the thread of the main work with OpenAI and allowed him to return to it with the information he had sought.Passages were revised three or four times  requests were rephrased  and responses were rejected. The work involved continually confronting the author's expectations with the AI system's proposals. The use of several AI systems served a single intellectual direction. Time saved on certain tasks gave him more time for decisions that shaped the book's meaning.This method is publicly declared: "" Human Thought & Dialogue: thierry | Writing: 100% AI  "" with the exceptions concerning quotations and archives specified in The Entrusted Pen  the prelude to the World-Book. The author sets out what he supplied  directed  revised  and rejected. Readers can compare this approach with the result  assess its merits  and discuss its implications.Word processors  proofreading tools  and search engines had already substantially changed the conditions of writing. Dialogue with AI adds the possibility of discussing a proposal and requesting an alternative based on an intention expressed in natural language. The time freed up can support more extensive research  allow the text to be examined anew  and make a more ambitious work feasible.The author's initiative can extend all the way to the encounter with readers. Digital technology has already opened up opportunities for visual artists to present their work  document their approach  and establish a direct relationship with their audiences. For thierry Ehrmann  AI extends this pursuit of autonomy in writing and the visual arts.Online bookselling now has a long history  dating back to the rise of online bookstores in the late 1990s. Self-publishing services are booming and flooding the Bibliothèque nationale de France with deposited publications. They enable authors to offer digital and printed books; authors can also arrange to disseminate them through their own websites. They can choose to sell their work  make it available free of charge  or adopt different arrangements for different editions. Bringing a book to public attention and developing a relationship with readers remains a task in its own right.With Dialogue Between a Thinker and AI   thierry Ehrmann chose to make the full text freely available in French and English. A profoundly humanistic  responsible path grounded in sustained experience. A path on which the human being and the algorithm illuminate one another  engage in dialogue  and build together without the human being relinquishing judgment.In thierry Ehrmann's words: ""Writing is not merely holding a pen. Authors bring a cosmovision : a singular way of perceiving the world through their experiences  knowledge  and contradictions. Whether writers or visual artists  they choose the means through which that thought takes form.The initial intention may become more precise as the work progresses. An objection  a discovery in the source material  or a new construction may lead authors to revise their starting point. Their freedom lies in their ability to decide on these transformations and recognize their meaning. In its role as a writing assistant  it does not pay their royalties  offer them a publishing advance  or decide whether their work will be distributed.
