The coverage spans NYSE  NYSE American  NYSE Arca  NYSE National and NYSE Texas.ADVERTISEMENTAccording to NYSE’s product page  BQT is a consolidated feed that combines 14 multicast channels into one product. It covers equities and ETFs listed on NYSE venues  Nasdaq and Cboe.The feed carries best bid and offer quotes with price and size  trades and closing prices from each primary market  and trades reported through the NYSE Trade Reporting Facility. It also includes market status messages for halts  resumptions  short-sale restrictions and session states  plus consolidated volume derived from the SIP trade feeds.NYSE says users receive the same information it sends to the Securities Information Processor  the public tape behind most stock quotes  at a lower cost.Pyth was careful about the scope. Tokenized versions of U.S. shares are spreading quickly across crypto venues. As we covered this week  OKXICE plans to open 24/7 tokenized stock trading on Nov. 23  and xStocks now covers every DAX 40 and FTSE 100 company.Every one of those products needs a trustworthy reference price. Pyth says venues using its Pyth Pro service to list equity perpetuals or tokenized stocks can show the official NYSE quote and last sale right beside the traded instrument.Regulators are moving in the same direction. On Nov. 3  CFTC Chairman Michael Selig announced that perpetual futures on individual equities can begin trading on certain dually registered U.S. exchanges. The agency will also host a roundtable on 24/7 derivatives trading on Dec. 15.How Did the Pyth Price React?Quietly.
