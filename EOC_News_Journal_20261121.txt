Its Pontes platform went live on September 21  2026  and settles wholesale tokenized securities transactions in central bank money.That last detail matters more than it sounds. The EU has long struggled to turn 27 national financial systems into something that behaves like a single capital market.Tokenization introduces a new version of that old problem. Different blockchain platforms can use different technical standards  different legal structures  and different settlement models. Early on  those differences are cheap to reconcile.
