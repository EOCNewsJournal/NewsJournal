Germany’s local cooperative banks account for 57 of the 78 entries dated Aug. 1 or later.The European Securities and Markets Authority refreshed its interim register of crypto-asset service providers on Friday  Nov. 20. Our count of that file shows 403 active firms across the EU and EEA  and 128 of them are supervised by Germany’s BaFin.Of those German entries  77 are Volksbanken  Raiffeisenbanken and other cooperative lenders. All of them are listed for one core service: executing crypto orders on behalf of clients.For Bitcoin investors  that matters more than another exchange licence. It means the bank on the town square is preparing to offer Bitcoin inside an app its customers already use.ADVERTISEMENTThe bigger question is whether this wave of small banks can turn regulatory filings into real retail demand while Bitcoin trades near two-year lows.What the MiCA Register ShowsESMA publishes the register as a downloadable data file on its MiCA page. We counted entries without an end date and removed duplicate legal entity identifiers.The pattern since summer is striking. Of the 78 entries dated Aug. 1 or later  57 are German cooperative banks  or 73%.November has followed the same script. Through Saturday  11 of the 16 entries dated this month are co-op banks  from Volksbank Ettlingen to Volksbank Oberberg.Three more are already queued with dates still ahead: Raiffeisenbank Kirchweihtal on Nov. 25  Raiffeisenbank Landshuter Land on Dec. 3 and VR-Bank Passau on Dec. 7.ADVERTISEMENTNew MiCA register entries per month  split between German cooperative banks and all other firms. June’s spike of 75 entries came almost entirely from other firms  just before MiCA’s longest transition window closed on July 1.The co-op wave arrived later  and it has been steady rather than rushed. That looks like a planned rollout  not a deadline scramble.Why Germany’s Co-op Banks Are Signing UpAll 77 co-op entries are filed under Article 60 of MiCA. That route lets already regulated credit institutions offer crypto services by notifying their supervisor  instead of applying for a separate crypto licence.The common thread is a shared platform. DZ BANK  the central institution of Germany’s cooperative banking group  said on Jan. 12 that BaFin had cleared it in late December 2025 to run a crypto platform called “meinKrypto”.According to DZ BANK  each Volksbank and Raiffeisenbank must then file its own MiCA notification before it can switch the service on for retail clients.The product is a wallet built into the VR Banking App. At launch it offers Bitcoin  Ether  Litecoin and Cardano  with custody by Boerse Stuttgart Digital and order execution through EUWAX AG.The platform was developed by DZ BANK and Atruvia  the group’s IT provider. Each bank decides on its own whether to join  and DZ BANK cited a September 2025 Genoverband survey in which more than a third of banks planned to introduce it.How Far Has the Rollout Reached?There is plenty of room left. Germany’s cooperative banking association BVR counted 646 independent cooperative banks at the end of 2025.That puts the 77 banks on the register at roughly 12% of the group. In other words  the wave so far is still only a first slice of the network.The terms are simple for customers. VR Bank Schleswig-Holstein Mitte  one of the listed banks  says users need to be adults resident in Germany with an account and the VR Banking App.The same bank says coins are held in omnibus wallets at Boerse Stuttgart Digital Custody  and it warns plainly that crypto holdings are not covered by deposit insurance.Active MiCA register entries by home country  with Germany split into cooperative banks and other firms. Infrastructure built during a drawdown is usually ready by the time sentiment turns.Europeans already lean toward Bitcoin when they own crypto. As we covered when the ECB surveyed euro area crypto holders  Bitcoin came out as the first choice.Large German banks are moving too. We recently looked at the partners behind Deutsche Bank’s Bitcoin custody  and the co-op network now adds a retail layer underneath.Still  a register entry is not a launch. Neither DZ BANK nor the listed banks have published user numbers  so we treat the filings as capacity  not proof of flows.Can Small Banks Bring the Next Wave of Bitcoin Buyers?For us  the key signal is the pace of new co-op entries in ESMA’s next updates.
